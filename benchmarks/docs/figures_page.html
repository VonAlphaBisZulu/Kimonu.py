F2

Concordance modules

ready · reference exhaustive 6 of 11

Kimonu vs COCOA across 33 is ready. Reference exhaustive (final rerun) is identical to Kimonu, partition for partition, on 6 landed models; iIT341 only after re-testing the 1300 pairs its scaling-bound defect had rejected (1286 re-accepted, partition then identical). The other 5 are running and get the same re-test on landing. Reference sampled is complete on 11 models × 3 seeds and is a strict subset of Kimonu's concordant pairs on every cell, never a superset, so its module count can land above or below Kimonu's (iIT341 178 vs 173, iLJ478 300 vs 267: dropped pairs split modules).

F3

Largest kinetic module

ready · reference on 6

Two Kimonu bars: Kimonu, and, starred, Kimonu with overlap-only module merging, which is the reference's merge rule. The four-phase (§S.2.2) and two-phase (Remark S2-1) upstream sets are identical seed for seed on all 33 models; the 8 models where the bars differ (iAM_P* family, iJR904, iSB619, iYO844) differ only by the Proposition S3-4 coupling merge that Kimonu and COCOA efficient=false apply and the reference code does not. The reference's own R-stage giants on the 6 landed final models equal Kimonu's on each. COCOA efficient=false overshoots on iJN678 (1183 vs 551) in every seed, and on iIT341 / iHN637 in one seed of three.

F4

ACR and ACRR (free)

ready · reference on 6

Every reference ACRR pair is in Kimonu's set by name on all six landed models; the reference's criterion is the combinatorial sufficient condition, Kimonu's is the kernel test. The tall COCOA efficient=false bars on iIT341 and iHN637 come from a single seed whose concordance cascaded (ACRR 2089 and 2211 against 20–24 and 116 in the other two seeds); iJN678's 2484 is stable across seeds.

F5

Runtime

ready

Reference times are concordance only, from the fixed bridge. Sampled cells took 0.2–53 h each; five of them (iIT341 ×2, Saha ×3) ran their prefilter at a 4 GB cgroup cap and swapped, inflating that phase by minutes, not hours. Right panel: Kimonu's kinetic stage dominates on every model because its exact concordance is nearly free — the inverse of the reference, which spends ≈100% in concordance.
